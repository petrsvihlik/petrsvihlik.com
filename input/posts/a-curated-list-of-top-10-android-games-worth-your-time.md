No matter whether you’re on a train, on a toilet, or on acid you’ll enjoy these games. Guaranteed!

![](/assets/img/posts/a-curated-list-of-top-10-android-games-worth-your-time/01.jpg)

What’s so special about them? They all follow a couple of principles:

1. The graphics are simple yet delightful and mostly 2D.
2. The rules and gameplay are easy to learn and remember but the game itself is often difficult to master.
3. The soundtracks are as important as the graphics and gameplay and are equally good.

OK, here goes my top 10 in no particular order:

## #1 Ninja Arashi series (1 & 2)

[This jump n’ run game](https://play.google.com/store/apps/developer?id=Black+Panther) of the modern era requires your absolute focus. It will reward you with precise controls that will never fail you.

## #2 Rusty Lake’s Cube Escape series

Eerie and surrealistic — that’s how the Dutch developers describe the world they’ve created. In this Van Gogh-styled [detective adventure](https://play.google.com/store/apps/dev?id=5341269538359321555), you’ll be uncovering mysteries, solving puzzles, and observe of obscure spectacles.

<div class="video" data-yt="R6RUyC-w9Lk" data-title="Cube Escape Collection - Official Trailer"><a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R6RUyC-w9Lk">Watch “Cube Escape Collection - Official Trailer” on YouTube</a></div>

## #3 Eufloria HD

Grow and colonize! You can no longer get this awesome strategy game on Google Play, however, it’s still available on [APK mirrors](https://www.apkhere.com/app/com.omnilabs.eufloria) and on [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/221180/Eufloria_HD/). And I highly recommend you to go grab it while it’s still there to enjoy the minimalistic soundtrack and pastel graphics.

<div class="video" data-yt="SVMgbIC2D8w" data-title="Eufloria HD Trailer"><a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SVMgbIC2D8w">Watch “Eufloria HD Trailer” on YouTube</a></div>

## #4 Alto’s series (Adventure & Odyssey)

This is an absolute chill-out. You can choose whether you prefer [snowboarding](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.noodlecake.altosadventure) or [sandboarding](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.noodlecake.altosodyssey). In any case, just lay in awe, enjoy the views, and let the time pass. And don’t forget to rescue the llamas. 🦙

<div class="video" data-yt="WGFpyjygANw" data-title="Alto’s Adventure - Trailer"><a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WGFpyjygANw">Watch “Alto’s Adventure - Trailer” on YouTube</a></div>

## #5 Ultraflow series (1 & 2)

Circles, triangles, and the sound of broken glass. Superfast, super fun [action puzzle](https://play.google.com/store/apps/dev?id=8078094304802054485). You won’t stop until you reach level 100!

<div class="video" data-yt="3Ad4EPC3Yys" data-title="ULTRAFLOW - Official Trailer"><a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3Ad4EPC3Yys">Watch “ULTRAFLOW - Official Trailer” on YouTube</a></div>

## #6 Lyne

Don’t be misled by this ingeniously simple concept, some levels can be really tricky to solve. While I was playing this game I had an urgent feeling that the ambient sounds must come from Half-life 2 or Mike Oldfield’s Discovery.

<div class="video" data-yt="MDQudjTzoLw" data-title="LYNE Trailer"><a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MDQudjTzoLw">Watch “LYNE Trailer” on YouTube</a></div>

## #7 Prune

If you’re looking for a [definition of an indie game](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.Polyculture.Prune), look no further. Plant a seed and follow the sun's rays.

<div class="video" data-yt="sfq7xXQlNOI" data-title="Prune - Launch Trailer"><a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sfq7xXQlNOI">Watch “Prune - Launch Trailer” on YouTube</a></div>

## #8 Mini Metro

Alright, you‘ve probably heard about this one. But if you’re a bit like me and avoid things that get too much promotion and win too many prizes, let me assure you — this one deserves all of it. Don’t be a fool like me and give this [metro simulation](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=nz.co.codepoint.minimetro) a try.

<div class="video" data-yt="Zbk_msu-iYI" data-title="Mini Metro Launch Trailer"><a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Zbk_msu-iYI">Watch “Mini Metro Launch Trailer” on YouTube</a></div>

## #9 Hidden Folks

A perfect way to get lost in time and space. [This game](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.adriaandejongh.hiddenfolks) will free you of any thoughts and grab 100% of your attention. Also, a perfect way to de-stress.

<div class="video" data-yt="kYw_tw__7ow" data-title="Hidden Folks - Official Trailer"><a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kYw_tw__7ow">Watch “Hidden Folks - Official Trailer” on YouTube</a></div>

## #10 Tsuki’s Adventure

Are you looking for a particularly relaxed game without any time constraints — a game that doesn’t require too much attention? This [Tamagotchi-style game](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.hyperbeard.tsuki) can not be played fast. In fact, you need to have it installed for months to make some serious progress. You’ll be rewarded with the cuteness of your animal friends and their silly dialogues. Let this game be the happy place that you can carry wherever you go.

<div class="video" data-yt="kyLZ649mG6I" data-title="Tsuki Adventure - Trailer"><a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kyLZ649mG6I">Watch “Tsuki Adventure - Trailer” on YouTube</a></div>

## #11 KAMI series (1 & 2)

Let’s see how good you are with origami. [KAMI](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.stateofplaygames.kami2) is a paper folding puzzle. If you’re into stationery and Japanese culture, this one is for you.
